Problem

Agentic Policies act open loop

A handle mislocated by a few millimetres, a rim clipped during extraction or a grasp that slips leaves no trace in the agent's state. In a pilot on LIBERO-Spatial with such an agent and no world model (29 episodes), task completion was 0.448, and the two contact-rich extraction tasks had no successes.

Our insight: contact needs its own subspace, its own asymmetric penalty around irreversible failure, and a differentiable energy the agent can descend before it actuates.

Central question. Under object-position and task shifts that break end-to-end VLAs, does an Agentic Policy grounded in a latent world model keep its success, and does the model's verification stay reliable?

Method

The agent proposes; A-HJEPA predicts, scores and refines

01

Agentic Policy

Gemini writes the robot program with perception and motion tools (SAM3, GraspNet, IK), merges three candidate programs into one, and re-generates over several turns. It proposes each action chunk and its joint goal.

02

Scene and contact tokens

A frozen V-JEPA 2 encoder turns the agentview and wrist cameras into tokens, joined by proprioception and force. Scene tokens carry objects, layout and arm; contact tokens carry wrist, proprioception and force.

03

Bi-level A-HJEPA

A block-causal predictor rolls the tokens forward at a 5 Hz micro step and a stride-4 macro step under end-effector delta actions, and predicts contact from the stop-gradient predicted scene.

04

Failure certificate, sample-and-select, recovery

A failure head on the predicted contact tokens scores the chance of a collision or a drop. A chunk under the threshold runs; otherwise 16 via-point paths to the same goal are scored in one rollout and the lowest is kept. A failure triggers recovery: interception, retreat, re-observation and re-synthesis.

The failure head is supervised and calibrated on training cells only; proprioceptive failure anchors are kept as an ablation.

Evaluation plan

A protocol built to attribute every gain

LIBERO-PRO from perception only: the Object, Goal and Spatial suites under the Pos and Task perturbations, six cells. Language-model proposals are cached and replayed, each ablation changes one component, and test failures are counted from simulator contact labels that neither the agent nor the world model sees.

H1 · RQ1

Verification holds under shift

On shifted cells, A2's failure score ranks chunks that end in a simulator-counted failure above the rest better than the stronger of a proprioception-only and an action-only control.

H2 · RQ2

The contact subspace helps

On the same cells, A2's failure AUROC exceeds the monolithic A1's, and its contact track probes contact better.

H3 · RQ3

Fewer failures in closed loop

With paired replay at an equal cap on language-model calls, A2 has fewer simulator-counted failures per episode than the agent without a world model, with no lower completion.

Each hypothesis is falsified if the 95% bootstrap interval of its difference contains zero. Results will be added once the runs are complete.
