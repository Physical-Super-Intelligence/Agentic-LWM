Problem

Code-as-policy agents act open loop

A handle mislocated by a few millimetres, a rim clipped during extraction or a grasp that slips leaves no trace in the agent's state. In a pilot on LIBERO-Spatial with such an agent and no world model (29 episodes), task completion was 0.448, and the two contact-rich extraction tasks had no successes.

Our insight: contact needs its own subspace, its own asymmetric penalty around irreversible failure, and a differentiable energy the agent can descend before it actuates.

Method

The agent proposes; A-HJEPA predicts, scores and refines

01

Dual-stream encoder with a contact gate

A shared vision backbone embeds the agentview and wrist cameras, and a small network embeds proprioception; a wrist-proprioceptive contact gate scales the visual features by a gate computed from proprioception.

02

Spatial / contact factorisation

The latent splits into a spatial track (object and arm SE(3) state) and a contact track made orthogonal to it by projection, so the contact track cannot become a second copy of the scene.

03

Bi-level A-HJEPA

A macro track predicts one stride of K=4 steps ahead; a micro track predicts one recorded step ahead and predicts contact causally from the predicted spatial state.

04

Latent energy, refinement and verified recovery

Before actuation, A-HJEPA certifies the agent's trajectory by the predicted stability of the contact track and, if certification fails, refines it by gradient descent on a latent energy. A grasp check from proprioception triggers recovery: interception, safe retreat, re-observation and re-synthesis.

Training uses a latent prediction loss plus a phase-margin loss whose failure anchors are mined from proprioception alone.

Evaluation plan

A protocol built to attribute every gain

LIBERO-PRO from perception only: the Object, Goal, Spatial and LIBERO-10 suites under the Pos and Task perturbations. Language-model proposals are cached and replayed, each ablation adds exactly one component, and failures are counted from simulator contact labels that neither the agent nor the world model ever sees.

Ablation ladder each row adds one component

RowVariant
A0A3 model, certify and raise only
A1Monolithic latent with refinement, equal capacity, trained end-to-end
A2+ spatial/contact factorisation (orthogonality loss, no phase margin)
A3+ phase-margin loss (full system)
H1 · RQ1

Factorisation helps contact

On the contact-rich subset, A2 completes more episodes than A1, and its contact track probes contact better.

H2 · RQ2

The margin prevents failures

A3 produces fewer drops and collisions per episode than A2 on LIBERO-PRO Spatial and Object (Pos).

H3 · RQ3

Refinement beats raising

At an equal cap on language-model calls, A3 completes more episodes than A0 over the eight cells.

Each hypothesis is falsified if the 95% bootstrap interval of its difference contains zero. Results will be added once the runs are complete.
